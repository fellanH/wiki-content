Place-Specific Dysphoria

condition

Place-specific dysphoria (PSD) is a broad psychological condition characterized by an intense, persistent, and often debilitating aversion to specific geographical locations, linked to temporal distortions and the lingering emotional residues of past events imprinted upon a place...

Also known as
PSD, Locational malaise, Geographic affect disorder
Classification
Psychological condition, IMPA Category III
Prevalence
~0.37% of global population
Key researchers
Dr. Silas Blackwood, Prof. Anya Volkov, Dr. Valeria Rossi
